# Funnel analysis

## Objective

Locate where a buying journey loses suitable prospects and identify the owner of the next investigation.

Impressions → Clicks → Leads → Qualified leads → Opportunities → Customers → Revenue

Counts become smaller, but each step should carry more commercial evidence. Use consistent cohort definitions; this month's leads should not be divided into this month's customers from older leads.

## Diagnostic framework

| Problem type | Evidence pattern | Next check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traffic problem | Wrong queries, companies or geographies | Targeting, search terms and audience promise |
| Conversion problem | Relevant visits, poor action completion | Message, offer, mobile, form and tracking |
| Lead quality problem | Many submissions, low fit or contactability | Offer expectations, qualification cues and source mix |
| Sales problem | Suitable leads, slow response or weak meeting progression | Ownership, follow-up, discovery and buying obstacles |

The pattern suggests an investigation, not a verdict. Poor opportunity conversion can also reflect product fit, price or a long sales cycle.

## Step-by-step process

1. Define stage entry, deduplication and outcome dates.
2. Group leads by acquisition period and source; allow time to mature.
3. Calculate adjacent-stage conversion and elapsed time between stages.
4. Compare with the same segment's previous mature cohorts.
5. Inspect records and customer feedback where the change first appears.
6. Assign one corrective action and a review date.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

RelayDesk's fictional cohort has 1,000 clicks, 100 leads, 40 qualified leads, 16 opportunities and eight customers. Click-to-lead is 10%; lead-to-qualified is 40%; qualified-to-opportunity is 40%; opportunity-to-customer is 50%.

If a later cohort has 100 leads but only 20 qualified, investigate traffic and offer quality. If it still has 40 qualified but only eight opportunities, inspect response times, discovery and evaluation barriers before changing ad copy.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Stage definitions and sources agree.
- [ ] Comparison uses mature, comparable cohorts.
- [ ] First weak transition is identified.
- [ ] Record-level evidence supports the diagnosis.
- [ ] Action is assigned to the relevant owner.

**Experiment idea:** improve the handoff response process for a controlled eligible lead group and measure meetings held, while holding acquisition treatment stable.
