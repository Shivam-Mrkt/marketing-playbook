# Conversion rate optimisation

## Objective

Increase valuable completed actions from the right audience. A higher form conversion rate can be a worse business result if it attracts more unsuitable leads.

## Metric framework

Conversion rate = completed target actions ÷ eligible visits or users × 100

Choose visits or users before analysis and keep the definition fixed. For a lead-generation test, qualified leads per eligible visitor can be the primary metric, while raw form completion diagnoses friction.

## Step-by-step process

1. Verify the event, denominator, duplicate rules and date window.
2. Segment results by source, intent and device to identify the actual constraint.
3. Gather qualitative evidence: form errors, buyer questions and observed confusion.
4. Write a hypothesis connecting one obstacle to one proposed change.
5. Define assignment unit, primary metric, guardrails and duration before launch.
6. Use a randomised comparison where possible and wait for downstream qualification.
7. Roll out, revise or retain the original based on evidence, including uncertainty.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

In a fictional scenario, page A receives 1,000 eligible sessions, 50 leads and 20 qualified leads. Page B receives 1,000 sessions, 70 leads and 14 qualified leads. Form conversion rises from 5% to 7%, but qualified-lead conversion falls from 2% to 1.4%. B is not a clear winner. This arithmetic alone does not establish statistical significance.

## Common mistakes

Stopping when one variant temporarily leads, comparing different traffic sources and removing every qualification cue can all produce misleading wins. Small samples may only support directional learning.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Primary action and eligible population defined.
- [ ] Hypothesis is tied to observed friction.
- [ ] Allocation remains consistent for the same visitor or account.
- [ ] Quality and technical guardrails included.
- [ ] Evaluation waits for the relevant outcome delay.

**Experiment ideas:** clarify demo expectations; compare a workflow image with a generic hero image; remove one form field whose answer never affects follow-up. Test these separately.
