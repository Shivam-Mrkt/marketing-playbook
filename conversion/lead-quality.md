# Lead quality

## Objective

Understand whether acquisition is creating relevant, reachable prospects with a plausible path to purchase. Quality needs explicit evidence and consistent sales feedback.

## Framework

| Dimension | Check | Typical action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Validity | Is this a real, non-duplicate submission? | Correct tracking, validation or reporting |
| Contactability | Can the person be reached through the appropriate channel? | Review form data and delivery |
| Fit | Can the business serve this account and use case? | Adjust audience or offer cues |
| Intent | Did they request evaluation or only education? | Match follow-up to readiness |
| Progression | Does a relevant conversation become an opportunity? | Inspect sales process and buying barriers |

## Step-by-step process

1. Agree acceptance and rejection reasons with sales.
2. Keep all generated leads visible, with invalid and duplicate exclusions reported explicitly.
3. Review a sample of accepted and rejected records by source weekly.
4. Separate poor fit from weak timing, unreachable contacts and missed follow-up.
5. Send quality themes back to targeting, creative, forms and bidding decisions.
6. Allow the qualification window before comparing recent sources with older ones.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

A fictional RelayDesk campaign attracts people seeking free spreadsheet templates. These can be useful education leads but may not be demo-ready. The team changes the commercial ad to name the software offer and clarifies the page's intended customer. It keeps the template programme separately measured instead of labelling all downloads as wasted traffic.

## Metrics

Qualified rate = qualified leads ÷ defined unique lead cohort. Cost per qualified lead = acquisition spend ÷ qualified leads from that cohort. Also track contactability, rejection reasons, opportunity rate and time to qualification.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Sales dispositions use shared definitions.
- [ ] Unreviewed leads are separate from rejected ones.
- [ ] Poor fit is separated from no current urgency.
- [ ] Optimisation considers quality and absolute volume.

**Experiment idea:** add one clear audience-fit statement to the page and measure qualified leads per visit. Do not declare victory from qualified percentage alone.
