# Lead qualification

## Objective

Confirm that a relevant problem and a workable buying conversation exist. Qualification should help the prospect and sales decide what to do next, not force every enquiry into an opportunity.

## Framework

| Area | Useful question | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fit | What team and systems will use this? | Service compatibility and constraints |
| Problem | What is happening in the current process? | Specific problem in the buyer's words |
| Impact | What makes this worth changing? | Business consequence; quantify only with evidence |
| Process | Who else needs to evaluate it? | Stakeholders and decision path |
| Timing | Why review it now? | Real trigger or absence of urgency |
| Next step | What would make a review useful? | Agreed action, owner and date |

## Stage rules

An MQL meets the scoring and fit gate. A High Intent flag prioritises an explicit request. An SQL requires sales-confirmed fit, a relevant problem and an agreed evaluation next step. An opportunity adds a defined active evaluation, accountable owner and recorded commercial context. Unknown budget can remain unknown; it must not be invented to complete a field.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

Fictional NorthstarOps requests a RelayDesk demo. Discovery confirms that two regional teams dispute lead ownership and that operations will review proposed rules with sales next week. The owner marks SQL and records the evaluation step. A student requesting a product overview receives educational material and a “research-only” disposition instead.

## Step-by-step process

1. Read the source, requested offer and known facts before contacting.
2. Ask only the questions needed to understand the situation.
3. Record evidence, unknowns and next action.
4. Accept, recycle or disqualify with a clear reason.
5. Return recurring rejection themes to marketing.

## Metrics and checklist

Track MQL acceptance, SQL-to-opportunity progression, response time and rejection reasons. Separate poor fit from good fit with no current timing; they need different treatment.

- [ ] Qualification is based on conversation or explicit evidence.
- [ ] Score has not substituted for sales judgement.
- [ ] Unknowns remain visible.
- [ ] Next step has an owner and date.

**Experiment idea:** standardise one discovery question about business impact and assess whether handoff notes become more actionable.
