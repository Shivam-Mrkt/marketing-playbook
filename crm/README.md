# CRM

The CRM should explain what is known about a lead, what stage they are in and who owns the next action. A database full of activity without clear decisions is not a working marketing system.

## Shared definitions

- **Lead:** a recorded person with a relevant source and contact context.
- **MQL:** meets the agreed fit and engagement gate; ready for review.
- **High Intent:** a priority flag for an explicit buying action; not a lifecycle stage.
- **SQL:** sales has confirmed a relevant need and agreed next step.
- **Opportunity:** an active evaluation with a defined problem, owner and next action.

Scoring prioritises review. It does not automatically confirm budget, authority or a real project.

**Illustrative example: B2B SaaS.** A suitable RelayDesk prospect downloads a guide and becomes an MQL after sufficient recent engagement. Another suitable prospect requests a demo and receives a High Intent flag immediately. Sales still qualifies both before marking either SQL.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Stage, score and priority flag are separate fields.
- [ ] Every active record has an owner or visible exception queue.
- [ ] Rejection reasons and timestamps are retained.
- [ ] Contact preferences apply across connected systems.
