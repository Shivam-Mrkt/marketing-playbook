# Content strategy

## Objective

Build a body of useful work that answers buyer questions, supports distribution and helps sales. The unit of planning is a customer problem, not a quota of blog posts.

## Framework

| Content job | Example | Success signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain a problem | Diagnostic guide | Relevant readers use the next resource |
| Help compare | Method comparison | Evaluation conversations reference it |
| Reduce purchase uncertainty | Implementation walkthrough | Fewer unanswered setup objections |
| Support adoption | Practical usage guide | Customers complete the relevant action |

## Step-by-step process

1. Gather recurring questions from customers, sales and search data.
2. Audit existing material: keep, improve, combine or retire. Preserve valuable destinations when changing URLs.
3. Prioritise topics by commercial relevance, missing evidence and distribution opportunity.
4. Assign a subject expert, writer, reviewer, owner and refresh trigger.
5. Create a brief with audience, intent, original contribution and CTA.
6. Plan distribution before drafting: sales use, newsletter, partners and social extracts.
7. Review by content purpose. An adoption guide should not be judged solely by new leads.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

Fictional RelayDesk builds a month around “lead ownership”: one detailed routing guide, one comparison page and one implementation worksheet. Sales uses the worksheet in discovery; the author turns the guide into a practitioner discussion. The team leaves room to update the product page if interviews reveal a more urgent objection.

## Metrics

Measure relevant organic visits, assisted evaluation journeys, qualified enquiries, sales usage and production cost. Record what content influenced a conversation without assigning it the full deal value.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Topic solves a documented buyer problem.
- [ ] Brief states what original value the page adds.
- [ ] Distribution has an owner and schedule.
- [ ] Claims have a source or expert review.
- [ ] Refresh is triggered by product, evidence or intent changes.

**Common mistake:** publishing more pages while useful existing pages remain inaccurate. **Experiment idea:** spend one publishing cycle improving a high-relevance page and compare its mature query cohort with an unchanged group.
