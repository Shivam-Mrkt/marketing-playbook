# Keyword research

## Objective

Choose search topics that connect customer needs to something the business can credibly offer. Volume is one input, not the prioritisation rule.

## Framework

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Relevance | Would the searcher plausibly become a suitable customer? |
| Intent | Are they learning, comparing or selecting? |
| Evidence | Do customer language, search results and query data agree? |
| Opportunity | Can we produce a meaningfully better answer? |
| Effort | Do we have the expertise and resources to maintain it? |

## Step-by-step process

1. Collect phrases from sales calls, support questions and customer interviews.
2. Expand with related searches and keyword tools. Treat volume and difficulty as estimates.
3. Inspect the results for the target market and device; record the date and dominant page types.
4. Group queries that share a task, not merely a repeated word.
5. Map each group to an existing or proposed page. Consolidate overlapping plans.
6. Rank opportunities high, medium or low using business relevance, evidence and effort. Keep the reasoning beside the score.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

For fictional RelayDesk, “lead routing software” has a direct product connection and needs a commercial page. “Round robin lead assignment” can support a practical guide with a product transition. “Sales quotes” is ambiguous and may have little relevance even if its estimated volume is large. The team prioritises the first two without claiming a ranking forecast.

## Metrics and common mistakes

Track relevant non-brand impressions, clicks, qualified organic enquiries and query-to-page alignment. A keyword's ranking without the right intent can be a distraction. Do not build one near-identical page for every wording variation or treat a third-party difficulty number as certainty.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Customer language included.
- [ ] Current results inspected in the intended market.
- [ ] Intent group has one clear page owner.
- [ ] Opportunity includes an evidence-based rationale.
- [ ] Business outcome is defined beyond rankings.

**Experiment idea:** improve an existing relevant page before creating a new URL for its synonyms; monitor the query group and qualified visits.
