# Landing-page SEO

## Objective

Build a commercial page that deserves to be found and makes evaluation easier. Search visibility and conversion work should share the same audience promise.

## Framework

Query intent → Clear offer → Evidence → Objections → Next step

## Step-by-step process

1. Choose the query group and confirm whether results support a commercial page.
2. Write a descriptive title and headline that name the solution naturally.
3. Explain the use case, audience and product limits in visible page content.
4. Add original proof: a real product walkthrough, approved customer evidence or a documented example. Do not invent testimonials.
5. Address buying questions such as implementation, compatibility, price approach and support.
6. Add useful internal links and check indexing, mobile experience and tracking.
7. Evaluate qualified organic enquiries alongside relevant query visibility.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

For fictional RelayDesk, the page targeting “lead routing software” opens with “Assign inbound leads by territory and ownership rules.” It shows a sample assignment flow, explains supported CRM connections only if verified, and states what the demo covers. An FAQ answers product questions drawn from actual research before publication.

The page links to a routing-method comparison for visitors not ready to evaluate a product. A paid-only promotional variant may serve a different job; do not create several indexable near-duplicates merely to repeat keywords.

## Metrics

Track non-brand organic clicks, qualified leads per organic session, CTA-to-form progression and opportunity creation by acquisition cohort. Keep paid and organic performance separate when their audiences differ.

## Common mistakes and checklist

Keyword repetition cannot compensate for missing product detail. Removing every explanatory paragraph to shorten the page can also remove what a serious buyer needs.

- [ ] Headline and search intent agree.
- [ ] Product claims have evidence.
- [ ] Buyer objections are answered plainly.
- [ ] Preferred URL and indexability checked.
- [ ] Mobile form and source capture tested.

**Experiment idea:** add a concise implementation section for queries suggesting setup concerns; monitor qualified conversions and query mix before drawing conclusions.
