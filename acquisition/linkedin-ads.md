# LinkedIn Ads

## Objective

Reach the buying roles and companies that matter, then judge the campaign by qualified conversations and pipeline. Professional targeting is valuable only when the resulting economics work.

## Targeting framework

| Input | Use | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job titles | Specific responsibilities | Equivalent titles and inconsistent profiles |
| Function and seniority | Reach a broader role family | Seniority does not establish buying authority |
| Industry | Narrow the market | Company classifications may be imperfect |
| Company size | Align with product and deal economics | Headcount is only a fit signal |
| Account targeting | Support an agreed ABM list | Match coverage is not complete account coverage |

Check the audience estimate and actual delivery after combining filters. A highly restrictive audience may not support the budget. Available targeting and formats should be checked in the [LinkedIn Ads guide](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/ads/ads-guide).

## Step-by-step process

1. Agree on target accounts or a firmographic segment with sales.
2. Map the practitioner, sponsor and approver; write a different reason to care for each.
3. Choose an offer appropriate to awareness: a useful diagnostic for a new audience, a workflow consultation for an evaluating one.
4. Match the format to the argument. Use single images for a focused claim, video for a demonstration, and documents for a framework someone can inspect.
5. Use thought leadership to present a substantive point of view. Thought Leader Ads can sponsor an individual's post with permission; verify format and objective compatibility. [LinkedIn's format guidance](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/lem/business/en/advertise/ads/getting-started/pdfs/LMS_ROI_Playbook_8-13-25.pdf).
6. Connect Lead Gen Forms to CRM ownership and follow-up. Prefilled information reduces effort, but the offer still needs to qualify interest. [Lead Gen Forms](https://business.linkedin.com/en-us/marketing-solutions/native-advertising/lead-gen-ads).
7. Retarget eligible engaged audiences with deeper proof. Exclude converted audiences when the next step no longer applies; avoid overexposing a tiny group.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

Fictional RelayDesk runs a six-week, $6,000 media pilot for revenue operations roles at 50–500 employee SaaS companies. Sales supplies an account list; marketing checks match coverage and creates one broader ICP segment for a separate comparison.

- **Offer:** an inbound routing diagnostic with sample territory rules.
- **Creative:** a document explaining common assignment gaps and a short product walkthrough.
- **Lead Gen Form:** name, work email, company and the routing issue they want to review.
- **Follow-up:** send the diagnostic, then offer a discussion relevant to the chosen issue.
- **Planning target:** 30 leads and 12 qualified leads; implied CPL $200 and qualified-lead cost $500. These are assumptions, not benchmarks.
- **Sales agreement:** review within one business day; record fit, problem and next step.

If form CPL falls but the proportion of suitable companies drops, tighten the offer or qualification before expanding. If an account engages but nobody accepts a meeting, inspect contact coverage and relevance before increasing frequency.

## Metrics and attribution

Track qualified-lead cost, meetings held, opportunities created and account progression. Report sourced pipeline separately from influenced pipeline, using named rules. A person seeing an ad before an opportunity closes does not establish that the ad caused the deal. Deduplicate account-level opportunities across contacts.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Target audience matches the sales motion.
- [ ] Creative offers substance before asking for a call.
- [ ] Form delivery and owner alerts tested.
- [ ] Matched-account coverage reviewed.
- [ ] Opportunity attribution window stated.
- [ ] Follow-up uses the requested offer, not an unrelated pitch.

## Experiment idea

Compare a diagnostic offer with a product consultation for the same eligible audience. Predefine cost per qualified meeting as the primary metric; hold budget and follow-up service level comparable.
