# Lead generation

## Objective

Turn relevant interest into a usable, permission-appropriate next step. A lead is a person who can be followed up with for a stated purpose; it is not automatically a sales opportunity.

## Offer framework

| Buying stage | Offer | Follow-up expectation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exploring a problem | Checklist or educational session | Deliver the resource; offer optional learning |
| Comparing approaches | Diagnostic or evaluation guide | Ask about the actual use case |
| Selecting a solution | Demo, consultation or trial | Route promptly to the right owner |

## Step-by-step process

1. Define a qualified lead with sales before setting a volume target.
2. Select an offer that solves a small, relevant problem.
3. Ask only for information needed to deliver, qualify or route. Explain the next step on the form.
4. Verify submission, duplicate handling, source capture and CRM delivery.
5. Give sales a response expectation appropriate to intent. A demo deserves different handling from a download.
6. Review contactability, rejection reasons and stage progression weekly.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

RelayDesk tests a “routing rules review” for teams already using a CRM. The form asks for work email, company and the current assignment challenge. The thank-you page explains the review process and offers scheduling. A student researching sales operations receives the educational resource but does not enter the sales queue.

Suppose a fictional channel produces 100 leads from $4,000 and another produces 40 from $3,000. If 10 and 20 respectively qualify, their qualified-lead costs are $400 and $150. The cheaper CPL channel is not the better qualified-demand source in this scenario.

## Metrics

Track valid unique leads, CPL, contactable rate, qualified rate, cost per qualified lead, meetings held and opportunities. Define which rejected records stay in the denominator; do not quietly remove poor leads to improve the rate.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Offer and form describe the same next step.
- [ ] Qualified-lead definition is shared.
- [ ] Test lead reaches a named owner.
- [ ] Duplicate and invalid submissions are visible.
- [ ] Source-specific rejection reasons inform optimisation.

**Experiment idea:** add one use-case question and measure qualified leads per eligible visitor, with form completion as a guardrail. A higher qualified percentage is not enough if qualified volume collapses.
