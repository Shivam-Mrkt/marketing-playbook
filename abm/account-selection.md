# Account selection

## Objective

Turn the ICP into a list the team can actually work. Prioritisation should reflect fit, value, timing and available capacity.

## Tiering framework

| Tier | Selection logic | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Strong fit, meaningful potential value, credible current signal | Individual account plan and sales partnership |
| 2 | Strong fit with a shared industry or problem | Small-group programme with role-specific messaging |
| 3 | Plausible fit, limited evidence of urgency | Broader education and periodic review |

Tier is an effort allocation, not a claim that an account will buy. Size the list from capacity: if an owner can research five accounts properly each week, a 200-account personalised launch is not a credible plan.

## Step-by-step process

1. Compile candidates from approved business sources and existing CRM records.
2. Deduplicate by company and identify parent/subsidiary relationships.
3. Apply must-haves and disqualifiers; flag unknowns for research.
4. Assess value using a plausible use case and deal range, not employee count alone.
5. Add dated timing evidence and known relationships.
6. Assign an owner, tier and next review date. Check open opportunities before outreach.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

Fictional NorthstarOps meets RelayDesk's size and workflow criteria and has publicly announced regional expansion. It enters Tier 1 for a short research sprint. Another suitable company with no current signal stays Tier 2. If the expansion has no effect on lead ownership, NorthstarOps can move down rather than remaining permanently urgent.

## Metrics and checklist

Track researched-account coverage, meaningful engagement, accepted meetings, opportunity rate and effort per progressed account. Compare tiers by their different treatments, not raw activity totals.

- [ ] Account is unique and fits the service model.
- [ ] Tier has a written reason.
- [ ] Sales conflicts checked.
- [ ] Owner has capacity and a next action.

**Experiment idea:** compare a signal-prioritised cohort with a fit-only cohort using similar account potential and effort. Treat non-random selection as a limitation.
