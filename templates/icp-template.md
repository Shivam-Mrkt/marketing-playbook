# ICP template

Use this to agree account fit across marketing, sales and customer success. Mark each criterion as required, preferred or contextual in the working copy. Keep fit separate from buying urgency.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

Provisional ICP for fictional RelayDesk; this has not been validated against customer outcomes.

| Field | Completed example | Decision rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Industry | B2B SaaS | Core target for the pilot |
| Company size | 50–500 employees | Preferred operating-complexity range |
| Revenue | Not required in the pilot; use verified revenue if available | Do not invent or block solely on missing revenue |
| Geography | US for the initial acquisition campaign | Confirm service coverage for exceptions |
| Technology | Supported CRM and inbound lead process | Verify compatibility before accepting fit |
| Business model | Sales-assisted recurring software revenue | Need for lead ownership must exist |
| Pain points | Disputed ownership, assignment exceptions, manual routing | Confirm in conversation |
| Buying triggers | Regional expansion or routing review | Timing hypothesis, not proof of need |
| Decision makers | Revenue operations, sales sponsor, relevant IT reviewer | Responsibilities require validation |
| Disqualifiers | No sales handoff or unsupported mandatory integration | Record a clear reason |
| Unknowns | Current routing rules and urgency | Research or ask; preserve unknown status |
| Owner and review | Demand generation lead with sales and customer success; monthly pilot review | Compare fit with opportunity progression and delivery effort |

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Must-haves reflect what the product can deliver.
- [ ] Preferences do not become unexplained exclusions.
- [ ] Poor-fit customers and lost deals informed the profile.
- [ ] Revision has an owner and effective date.
