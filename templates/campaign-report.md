# Campaign report template

Use to make a decision, not just recap activity. State acquisition period, outcome cutoff, currency, cost scope and attribution method before comparing performance.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

Fictional RelayDesk campaign. All counts are invented, with a 30-day acquisition cohort reviewed after a 90-day outcome window. Currency is USD. Revenue uses a stated last-recorded-campaign convention and is deduplicated in the CRM; it is not a causal estimate.

| Field | Completed example | Definition / interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Objective | Evaluate commercially viable qualified demand | Assess full cohort after sales progression |
| Spend | $12,000 | Media spend; full campaign investment is $15,000 |
| Reach | 100,000 | Platform-reported unique people; not cross-platform deduplicated |
| Impressions | 200,000 | Total ad displays |
| Clicks | 2,000 | Platform-recorded clicks |
| CTR | 1% | 2,000 ÷ 200,000 |
| CPC | $6 | $12,000 ÷ 2,000 |
| Leads | 100 | Unique submitted leads under the declared duplicate rule |
| CPL | $120 | $12,000 ÷ 100 |
| Qualified leads | 40 | Shared fit and qualification definition |
| Cost per qualified lead | $300 | $12,000 ÷ 40 |
| Opportunities | 20 | Deduplicated active evaluations |
| Customers | 10 | New customers from this acquisition cohort |
| Revenue | $30,000 | Recognised revenue within the defined outcome window |
| ROAS | 2.5× | Revenue ÷ media spend |
| ROI | 60% | ($24,000 contribution − $15,000 campaign investment) ÷ $15,000 |

Contribution is revenue less $6,000 variable delivery costs. Campaign investment includes media plus $3,000 allocated acquisition people, creative and tools. Fixed company overhead is outside this calculation. See the [ROI playbook](../analytics/roi-analysis.md) for cost boundaries.

## Insights

The scenario produces positive attributed contribution after campaign investment. It does not show whether more budget would preserve efficiency or whether every attributed customer was incremental. Ten customers is a limited basis for a scaling forecast.
