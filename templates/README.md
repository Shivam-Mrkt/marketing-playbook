These templates turn a working decision into a brief, plan or review. Each includes instructions and a completed **Illustrative example**. The fictional entries are teaching material, not Shivam's results.

## How to use them

1. Copy the relevant document into your campaign workspace.
2. Retain the field names and replace the illustrative entries with verified campaign information.
3. For a genuinely unknown fact, record the unknown, its owner and how it will be resolved. Do not invent an answer to complete a table.
4. Remove the teaching instructions from the working copy if they are no longer needed.
5. Assign an owner and review date, then link the final report or next decision.

## Handoff checklist

- [ ] Example facts have been replaced or clearly retained as illustration.
- [ ] Assumptions and evidence are distinguishable.
- [ ] Metrics, costs and dates have consistent definitions.
- [ ] The next action is clear to someone outside the planning meeting.
