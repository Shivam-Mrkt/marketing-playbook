# Campaign brief template

Use before creative production or media setup. Each row should make a decision that another person can execute. Replace the example entries in a working copy; keep assumptions explicit.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

RelayDesk is fictional. Targets are planning assumptions, not forecasts or achieved results.

| Field | What to record | Completed example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Objective | Business outcome and period | Generate 40 qualified demo leads in a 30-day acquisition cohort |
| Audience | Fit, role, geography and intent | Revenue operations at 50–500 employee US B2B SaaS firms searching for routing software |
| Offer | Exchange and next step | Demo using a sample inbound routing workflow |
| Channel | Channel's specific job | Google Search captures category and use-case intent |
| Messaging | Problem, promise and approved proof | Explain territory assignment with a sample rule; no unsupported savings claim |
| Budget | Cost scope and allocation | $12,000 media: $1,000 brand, $9,000 non-brand, $2,000 controlled Search test |
| KPIs | Primary, diagnostics and guardrails | $300 per qualified lead; 40 qualified leads; CPL and form errors as diagnostics |
| Timeline | Preparation, launch and review | Week 0 QA; days 1–30 acquisition; day 44 quality review; later sales-cohort review |
| Owner | Accountable role and partners | Demand generation lead; paid media manager executes; sales lead owns handoff |
| Measurement | Sources, events and attribution | Successful unique form events; CRM qualification; acquisition-cohort reporting; brand separated |
| Dependencies | What must work before launch | Form delivery, owner coverage, conversion tracking and approved page claims |
| Decision rule | What triggers the next action | Hold scale if qualified share deteriorates; review mature quality before reallocating |

## Handoff checks

- [ ] Sales agrees the qualification definition and response SLA.
- [ ] Creative and landing-page promises match.
- [ ] Budget ceiling includes a clear cost scope.
- [ ] Test and non-test activity can be distinguished.
- [ ] Launch owner has completed the full enquiry journey.
