# Marketing Playbook

A practical collection of marketing frameworks, experiments, playbooks and templates by Shivam. This repository documents how I approach acquisition, demand, conversion and the systems that connect marketing activity to business outcomes.

## What this repository is

A working reference for making marketing decisions: who to reach, what to offer, how to execute, what to measure and when to change direction. Each playbook includes a process, an example and checks that can be used in real campaign planning.

This is a portfolio of marketing thinking. Company scenarios, budgets and experiment results are explicitly illustrative; they are not claims about my client work or achievements.

## Areas of focus

- **Acquisition:** performance marketing, Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, AI-assisted advertising, demand generation and lead generation.
- **Organic growth:** SEO, search intent, content strategy and AI search visibility.
- **B2B growth:** ABM, outbound, account research and buying committees.
- **Marketing operations:** CRM, lead scoring, segmentation and automation.
- **Commercial performance:** conversion optimisation, lead quality, analytics and experiments.

## Experiments

The [experiment framework](experiments/experiment-framework.md) follows a simple sequence:

Problem → Hypothesis → Change → Metric → Test → Result → Learning → Next action

The examples show how to interpret mixed and inconclusive evidence, as well as promising results. Every numerical outcome is labelled **Illustrative example**.

## Templates

Start with a [campaign brief](templates/campaign-brief.md), use the relevant playbook, then close the loop with a [campaign report](templates/campaign-report.md). Templates include working instructions and completed fictional examples so they can be adapted without guessing what belongs in each field.

## Marketing philosophy

- Start with the buyer's problem and the commercial constraint.
- Choose channels for a specific job. Being present everywhere is not a strategy.
- Judge lead generation by what happens after the form submission.
- Keep measurement definitions stable enough to compare decisions fairly.
- Automate repeatable work after the process works manually.
- Use AI to widen the options; verify evidence and own the final choice.
