# Campaign analysis

## Objective

Explain performance well enough to make the next decision. Reporting a percentage change without identifying the affected audience or funnel stage rarely helps.

## Step-by-step process

1. State the decision: hold, scale, fix, test or stop.
2. Reconcile spend, conversions and CRM stages. Check date boundaries, duplicates and data delays.
3. Compare mature acquisition cohorts or clearly separate early indicators from later outcomes.
4. Break down by channel, brand/non-brand, audience, offer and device where volume supports it.
5. Find whether the change comes from traffic cost, conversion, qualification or sales progression.
6. Review campaign changes and buyer feedback for plausible explanations.
7. Recommend one next action with an owner, budget implication and review date.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

Fictional RelayDesk compares two fully reviewed acquisition cohorts; these are invented teaching figures.

| Metric | Cohort A | Cohort B |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Spend | $6,000 | $6,000 |
| Leads | 60 | 100 |
| CPL | $100 | $60 |
| Qualified leads | 24 | 20 |
| Qualified rate | 40% | 20% |
| Cost per qualified lead | $250 | $300 |

**Observation:** CPL improved while qualified-lead economics worsened. **Hypothesis:** broader traffic or a weaker qualification cue changed the mix. **Next check:** inspect query groups and rejection reasons. **Decision:** hold budget until the quality change is understood; do not scale purely on CPL.

## Metrics and common mistakes

Use CTR and CPC to diagnose delivery, CPL for lead efficiency, qualified-lead cost for acquisition quality, and opportunities/customers for commercial progression. Do not blend currencies or compare a seven-day-old lead cohort with a fully mature one. Aggregate rates can change because segment mix changed even when each segment stayed stable.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Data is complete enough for the decision.
- [ ] Comparison populations are comparable.
- [ ] Facts and possible causes are separated.
- [ ] Business effect is quantified where supported.
- [ ] Recommendation names the next check or action.

**Experiment idea:** if query mix is the suspected driver, isolate one intent group for a controlled change instead of rewriting the whole campaign.
