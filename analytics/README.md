# Marketing analytics

Clicks are not the final objective. Analytics should show how activity becomes qualified demand, customers and revenue, and where the evidence is incomplete.

Impressions → Clicks → Leads → Qualified leads → Opportunities → Customers → Revenue

## Measurement agreement

Before a campaign begins, name the source of truth for spend, lead stages and revenue. Agree on currency, timezone, cohort dates, duplicate handling and attribution window. Preserve stage timestamps so later sales outcomes can be connected to the right acquisition cohort.

**Illustrative example: B2B SaaS.** RelayDesk's ad platform reports form conversions, its CRM reports accepted leads and finance reports realised revenue. These totals answer different questions. The review reconciles definitions instead of forcing every system to show the same count.

## Checklist

- [ ] Primary business question appears above the dashboard.
- [ ] Metric definitions include denominator and period.
- [ ] Immature outcomes are marked as pending.
- [ ] Pipeline and revenue are separate.
- [ ] Each review ends with an owner and next action.
