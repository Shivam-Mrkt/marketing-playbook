# Multichannel outreach

## Objective

Use channels for complementary jobs while keeping one coherent conversation. Adding channels should improve relevance or access, not multiply pressure.

## Framework

Account plan → Shared owner → Appropriate channel → Response recorded → Next action updated

| Channel | Useful job | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email | Explain a relevant offer asynchronously | Clear identity and easy decline |
| LinkedIn | Professional context and discussion | No assumption that connection means purchase intent |
| Paid media | Support education across an eligible audience | Exposure does not establish individual engagement |
| Phone | A timely conversation where appropriate and permitted | Check preferences and calling requirements |
| WhatsApp | Expected follow-up after opt-in | Not a fallback cold channel when email is unanswered |

## Step-by-step process

1. Check existing relationships and choose one account owner.
2. Assign each channel a job and a shared maximum cadence.
3. Record all touches and replies in the CRM.
4. Check current status before each action; stop parallel sequences on response.
5. Hand a positive conversation to the right person with full context.
6. Review account-level outcomes so multiple contacts do not inflate results.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

RelayDesk emails fictional NorthstarOps a territory-rules question on day 1. On day 5 it shares one practical rule example. A relevant LinkedIn interaction on day 10 adds professional context. If Maya replies on day 6, the day-10 scheduled outreach stops and the owner continues the actual conversation.

**Bad example:** emailing, calling and messaging three employees on the same day with identical pitches. Channel variety does not create new value.

## Metrics and checklist

Track qualified conversations per account, meetings held, total touches before response and negative feedback. Attribute the journey once at opportunity level rather than crediting each channel with a separate deal.

- [ ] One owner sees all activity.
- [ ] Channel has a distinct purpose.
- [ ] Global suppression overrides schedules.
- [ ] Existing opportunities follow the sales plan.

**Experiment idea:** compare email-only with email plus one relevant professional-network touch at account level, holding the offer and total effort measurable.
