# Personalised outreach

## Why it matters

Personalisation should explain why the offer is relevant. Mentioning a hobby or repeating a company slogan rarely improves the business case.

## Framework

| Level | Input | Useful adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Segment | Shared operating problem | Offer and terminology |
| Role | Responsibility and likely question | Argument and CTA |
| Account | Verified business change | Timing and context |
| Individual | Relevant public professional contribution | A specific follow-up question |

Spend effort in proportion to account value and available evidence. Good segment personalisation can be more useful than shallow individual trivia.

## Step-by-step process

1. Write the shared problem and offer for the segment.
2. Add one verified account fact and record its source/date internally.
3. Explain the possible implication without stating it as known pain.
4. Adapt the next step to the person's role.
5. Read the message without the company name. If it says nothing specific, improve the logic rather than adding more compliments.
6. Review any AI-produced context before sending.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

**Good:** “NorthstarOps is adding a European sales team. Does that change how you assign cross-region enquiries? The territory-rules checklist may help surface overlaps before the rollout.”

**Bad:** “I love NorthstarOps' mission and saw you enjoy hiking. Our solution can transform your business.” The details do not establish a relevant need.

For a practitioner, offer a rules checklist. For a sponsor, ask whether inconsistent ownership is affecting a current priority. Neither message should invent internal losses.

## Metrics and checklist

Measure qualified replies per research hour, accepted meetings and factual error rate. A highly personalised email that takes an hour and produces no useful response is not automatically a better process.

- [ ] Context is true and recent enough.
- [ ] Business implication is explicit.
- [ ] Unconfirmed need is a question.
- [ ] CTA matches the role and evidence.

**Experiment idea:** test segment-only against account-context personalisation in comparable accounts. Keep offer and sequence constant.
