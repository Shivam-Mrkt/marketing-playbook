# LinkedIn outreach

## Objective

Start a professional conversation that fits the recipient's role and context. A connection acceptance is not consent to a long automated pitch sequence.

## Process

1. Check role relevance, recent public professional context and existing account ownership.
2. Engage with a post only when there is a substantive contribution to make.
3. Send a concise connection note where appropriate; avoid packing in the full offer.
4. After acceptance, ask a relevant question or offer a useful resource.
5. Respond to the person's answer rather than continuing a fixed script.
6. Stop after a bounded unanswered follow-up or any decline. Use methods consistent with the platform's current rules.

## Illustrative example: B2B SaaS

Fictional context: Maya at NorthstarOps has posted about territory planning.

**Connection note:** “Maya, your post on regional sales ownership raised a useful question about overlapping territories. I work on lead-routing problems and would be interested in following your perspective.”

**After acceptance:** “Thanks, Maya. When territories overlap, does your team resolve ownership through account rules or manager review? I have a short comparison of both approaches if useful.”

**One later follow-up:** “One distinction in the comparison: account rules can reduce repeated decisions, but exceptions still need an owner. Happy to share it; otherwise I'll leave it here.”

**Bad example:** “Thanks for connecting! Here's my calendar, deck and a five-paragraph pitch.” It treats acceptance as buying intent and creates work before offering value.

## Qualification and metrics

Look for a relevant problem, role and willingness to take a next step. Track substantive replies, qualified conversations and meetings held. Connection rate is a diagnostic, not the business outcome. Do not infer buying intent from profile views alone.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Context was actually read and verified.
- [ ] Message fits the role.
- [ ] No invented common connection or praise.
- [ ] Account owner sees the conversation.
- [ ] Declines and channel preferences are respected.

**Experiment idea:** compare a context-led question with a relevant resource offer. Keep account type comparable and evaluate conversation quality, not acceptance alone.
